## About

Staff web developer and platform architect with over fifteen years of experience building enterprise web platforms and the teams that deliver them. Through successive roles as tech lead, platform architect, and organizational right hand, I have worked across global pharmaceutical enterprises, government, agencies, and a technology company I co-founded.

Currently a Technology Senior Associate at Appnovation Technologies, leading development teams on enterprise engagements for Pfizer. Seeking a move into engineering management or a director-level role.

Published complexity scientist and urbanist. Author of [Emergent Urbanism](http://emergenturbanism.com). Regular contributor to [The Nature of Cities](https://www.thenatureofcities.com/author/mathieuhelie/).
