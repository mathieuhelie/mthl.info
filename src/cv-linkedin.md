## About

Web developer by trade, complexity scientist by passion, entrepreneur when called. I build web platforms and applications with Drupal, PHP, and JavaScript, and I write about the intersection of complexity science and urban planning.

Currently a Technology Senior Associate at Appnovation Technologies. Co-founder of Agence Webdiffusion. Published author at [The Nature of Cities](https://www.thenatureofcities.com/author/mathieuhelie/). Creator of [Emergent Urbanism](http://emergenturbanism.com).

📍 Montreal, Canada · 📧 mthl@mthl.info
